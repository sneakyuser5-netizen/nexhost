Frequently Asked Questions

What is NEXHOST?

NEXHOST is a Node.js bot hosting platform that lets users create, upload, start, stop and manage their bot applications.

How do I create a bot?

Open My Bots, select Create Bot, enter a bot name and entry file, then create your application.

How do I upload my bot files?

Open your bot and use the Upload ZIP option to upload its source files.

Which runtime does NEXHOST support?

The current hosting engine supports Node.js applications.

How can I change my password?

Open Account, find Change Password, enter your current password and choose a new password.

What if I forget my password?

Use Forgot Password on the login screen and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

Can I monitor my bots?

The dashboard provides bot status, deployment history and resource-monitoring features where available.
